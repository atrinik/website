# Provenance

All website source, prose, styles, schemas, tests, and fixtures introduced by
this repository are new MIT-licensed work written for the clean-room
replacement. They use public issue requirements, public platform
documentation, and generic interoperability facts.

The exact public Internet Archive snapshot of atrinik.org at
<https://web.archive.org/web/20160307051707/https://www.atrinik.org/> was
reviewed only for historical information architecture and project context. No
archived website code, theme, prose, or media was copied or adapted. The
website's Morliana copy is a fresh paraphrase informed by the Morliana story in
`maps/books.art` from `atrinik/content` branch `1.x` at revision
`2796dee0b9ac5337bd8b10dd591da124319ffea3`; no passage or content file was
copied, and that repository remains the authoritative source.

The four original PNG files under `artwork/originals/` were generated at Zoey
Rose's direction in an earlier OpenAI image-generation session. Zoey received
the outputs, selected these four, and contributed them under this repository's
MIT terms. They are credited to “Zoey Rose with OpenAI image generation.” The
WebP derivatives under `public/media/` are bound to their source paths,
immutable Git blob object IDs, source and published digests, published
dimensions, transformations, license, notices, and alternative text in
`src/data/media.json`.

The synthetic download and media records under `tools/` are contract-only test
data. They do not name or contain a real release or asset and are never
published by Astro. `src/data/downloads.json` remains empty until exact
externally owned evidence is reviewed.

Future reuse under an approved historical grant must follow the exhaustive
registry and proof requirements in `atrinik/atrinik` and record the exact root
registry revision. This file does not maintain a grantor list or imply a
blanket grant.
