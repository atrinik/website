# Provenance

All source, prose, styles, schemas, tests, and fixtures introduced by this
repository are new MIT-licensed work written for the clean-room replacement.
They use public issue requirements, public platform documentation, and generic
interoperability facts. No classic website code, theme, prose, media, tests,
or private project material was consulted or copied.

The synthetic download and media records under `tools/` are contract-only test
data. They do not name or contain a real release or asset and are never
published by Astro. `src/data/media.json` and `src/data/downloads.json` are
empty until exact externally owned evidence is reviewed.

Future reuse under an approved historical grant must follow the exhaustive
registry and proof requirements in `atrinik/atrinik` and record the exact root
registry revision. This file does not maintain a grantor list or imply a
blanket grant.
